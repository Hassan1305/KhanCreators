How we work

Small team.
Short distances.

With two of us, the person who hears your idea is the person who builds it. Nothing gets lost in translation, and decisions take minutes, not meetings.

Listen

We start with what you're trying to fix, not a feature list, and say plainly what's worth building first.

Build in the open

Working builds early and often, on your own phone or screen, so you steer while there's still time to.

Ship properly

Store listings, reviews, privacy pages and the hundred small details that turn a build into a release.

Stay

After launch we fix, improve and keep things running. We still look after everything we've shipped.
