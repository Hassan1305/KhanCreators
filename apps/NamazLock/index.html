FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What happens if I miss a prayer?

The lock releases on its own when that prayer's window ends, so your phone is never held hostage. The prayer shows as missed in your history, and you can log it later as qada.

Does it check that I actually prayed?

No, and it never will. Tapping “I prayed” is enough. Namaz Lock is a nudge to put the phone down, not a judge.

What if I can't pray right now?

Snooze it for 5 or 15 minutes, or until the window ends. Women can turn on excused days to pause blocking during menstruation or postpartum without losing their streak.

Which apps can I lock?

Any you choose. Most people pick social media, video and games. Calls, messages and maps stay available unless you add them yourself.

What permissions does it need?

On Android: usage access and display over other apps, so it knows when a chosen app opens and can cover it. On iPhone: Screen Time. The app walks you through each one. Location is used only to calculate prayer times.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on the device. An account is only for optional backup and sync with Plus.
