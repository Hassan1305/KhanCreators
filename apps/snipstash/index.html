Questions

Before you buy

Is this really a one-time purchase?

Yes. $19.99 once, on every device signed in to your Apple Account. There is no subscription, and there is no plan to add one.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. A native Mac app is in the works.

Where do my snippets live?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if you turn sync on. We run no servers, so there is nowhere for us to store or read anything.

The keyboard needs Full Access. Why?

iOS requires Full Access for a custom keyboard to reach your saved snippets at all. SnipStash's keyboard contains no networking code, so nothing you type can leave your device through it.

Who makes SnipStash?

Two people, Hassan and Bilal. It's young, and the roadmap is genuinely being shaped by the people using it. Write to us and you'll get a reply from one of us.
