Most apps say they respect your privacy. Here is exactly what SnipStash does, so you can check it yourself.

Read the full policy

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No servers of ours. Sync runs through your own iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • No analytics SDK, no tracking, no ads.
  • The keyboard contains no networking code at all. It never records what you type, it only reads the snippets you saved.
