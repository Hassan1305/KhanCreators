New · Templates

Send the whole message,
not the pieces.

A template is a saved message made of several parts: text, images and files, kept in the order you send them. Welcome a new client, send a quote with its price list, share directions with a map photo. Build it once, send it in one go.

  • Blocks, in orderText, image, text, PDF is four blocks. Drag to reorder, insert a block wherever you are. Same editor on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • { }{Variables}, no setupType {Client name} in any text block and it becomes a fill-in. SnipStash asks for the values when you send, prefilled from last time. Works on the free plan.
  • Four ways to sendCopy as rich text with the images inline. Share through the share sheet. Email, with files as real attachments. Or send it step by step.
  • Step by step, for chat appsWhatsApp, Telegram and Messages will not take text and pictures in one paste. SnipStash hands you one block at a time, so the order survives.

Three starter templates are ready to open pre-filled the first time you look: welcome a new client, send a quote, share directions. Templates sync through your own iCloud, show up in search and Spotlight, and sit in the Mac palette. The keyboard can type a template's text; its images and files come from the app.
