Frequently asked questions

Does Morning Line require an internet connection?
No. Morning Line works fully offline. All quotes are bundled with the app.

Does Morning Line collect any of my data?
No. Morning Line does not collect, store, or share any personal data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Morning Line show ads?
No. Morning Line has no ads and never will.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?
Email us at ch.hasni.khan@gmail.com with a description of the issue or idea. Screenshots are always helpful if you're reporting a bug.

How do I delete my data?
Morning Line doesn't collect or store any data on our servers, so there's nothing to delete on our end. To remove any local app preferences, simply uninstall the app from your device.
