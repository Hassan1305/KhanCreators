Frequently asked questions

Does Morning Line require an internet connection?
No. All quotes are bundled with the app and the daily notification is scheduled on your device. Ads are the only part that uses the network.

Does Morning Line collect any of my data?
Morning Line has no accounts and no servers: your favorites, reading position and settings stay on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them, and RevenueCat records a Remove ads purchase. Our Privacy Policy lists exactly what.

Does Morning Line show ads?
Yes. The app is free, with a small banner at the bottom and one full-screen ad that appears only if you choose "Keep scrolling" when the app suggests a break. A one-time Remove ads purchase in Settings removes all ads for good. Use Restore purchases in Settings on a new device.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?
Email us at ch.hasni.khan@gmail.com with a description of the issue or idea. Screenshots are always helpful if you're reporting a bug.

How do I delete my data?
We have no servers and hold no data about you. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. For data held by Google (ads) or RevenueCat (purchases), see the links in our Privacy Policy, or email us.
