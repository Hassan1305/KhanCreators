Questions

Asked,
answered.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every quote is bundled in the app, and the daily notification is scheduled on your phone. Ads are the only part that uses the network.

Will I see the same quote twice?

Not for a long time. Each deck is shuffled once and dealt in order, so every quote in it comes up before any repeats. Your place is saved after every quote, so closing the app doesn't reset anything.

What data does it collect?

Morning Line has no account and no servers. Your favorites, reading position and settings stay on your phone. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them. The privacy policy lists exactly what.

Is it on Android?

Android, with its home-screen widget, is in closed testing now. Email us to join, or check back soon.

Can I share a quote?

Yes, from the share button under any quote. Favorite the ones that land and they're kept in one list.
