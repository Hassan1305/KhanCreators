Privacy Policy — Quotes: Morning Line

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Morning Line ("the app") is developed by Hassan Nawaz Khan ("we", "us", "our").

Summary

Morning Line does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account creation, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no network connection required to use the app. All content and any preferences you set stay on your device.

Data we collect

None. Morning Line does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or any other data from you. The app functions entirely offline and does not communicate with any server.

Data sharing

Since we do not collect any data, we have nothing to share with third parties.

Children's privacy

Morning Line does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not pose data-privacy risks to users of any age.

Third-party services

Morning Line does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that involves data collection), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
